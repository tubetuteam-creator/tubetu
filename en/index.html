For parents

What our cartoon is about

TUBETU means "to be two" — being together. We tell children that together you can do more, and kindness always comes back.

Friendship and teamwork

The heroes are different, but they win only together.

Help without reward

The team flies to anyone who calls — even to a greedy villain.

Laughter and kind villains

Our villains are funny, not scary. Lots of humour, no cruelty.
