Episode 1. “Treasure Rock”

Little Miroshka climbed into an ice crevasse after his baby goat — and got stuck. Greedy Skupshchik and his buzzing drones are already circling above: they want the sparkling crystals. Lisko, Dobrik, Vionka and Asila fly out of the Treasure Rock to help. Will they make it in time — and what will Skupshchik choose when he has to choose between a crystal and a life?

The team's friends

No adventure happens without them.

Dyusha

Parrot navigator and courier
Always flies ahead and mixes up left and right. “Handsome!”

Nyukh

A dog with a magic nose
His nose glows like a GPS. Loves pizza and everyone in the world.

Kvasya

Knitted teddy bear with a sweet tooth
A quiet sweetheart: he drops everything — but always at the right moment. His power is the “lucky fall”.

Shushik

Chinchilla engineer
Busy and grumpy. Knows every tunnel in the Rock and sneezes from dust.

Skot

Cat, head of HQ
Stylish and clever. Gives advice (not always right) and is afraid of getting dirty.

Ukh

Little owl, guard of the Rock
Announces every episode… if he doesn't fall asleep on duty.

Snezhok

Snow leopard cub from the snowy mountains
He doesn't talk — he purrs, snorts and sneezes. But he knows every mountain path.
Funny villains

Skupshchik's gang

Our villains are funny, not scary. They want to grab everything for themselves — and get into trouble every time.

Skupshchik

A sly greedy guy in a long coat. The coins in his pockets squeak like rubber ducks. His word is “Mine!”, and his weakness is anything free.

Bugor

A huge good-natured boar porter. “The boss said — I carry.” Ready to turn good for a treat.

Buzzers

Thief drones: “Bzzz! Grab!” They love music — Asila's song makes them dance.

The world of TUBETU

Where the team lives

High in the mountains stands the Treasure Rock. Its door opens only for friends — and inside there's a huge HQ with a glass dome, tunnels and a sweet corner.

Drawing contest

Draw your favorite TUBETU hero!

Lisko, Dobrik, Vionka, Asila, Dyusha, Nyukh, Kvasya, Ukh, Snezhok — draw the one you like best. Pencils, paints, markers — however you like!

1. Draw

Your favorite hero or the whole team. You can imagine where they'll fly next time.

2. Send it

Parents post a photo of the drawing on Instagram or TikTok with the hashtag #TUBETUdrawing or send it to our Telegram chat.

3. Get a gift

The best works will get TUBETU certificates and gifts — calendars with the heroes and surprises. We'll show the brightest drawings right here on the website.

For parents: by sending a drawing, you agree that we may show it on the TUBETU website and social media with the child's first name and age. We never publish surnames, addresses or photos of children. Results — in our social media.

Parents

What our cartoon is about

TUBETU means “to be two” — being together. We tell children that together you can do more, and kindness always comes back.

Friendship and teamwork

The heroes are different, but they win only together — each with their own strength.

Help without reward

The team flies to anyone who calls — and even offers a hand to a greedy villain.

Laughter and kind villains

Our villains are funny, not scary. Lots of humor, no cruelty.
